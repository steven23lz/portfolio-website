IT Student. System Builder. Problem Solver.

I am an IT student at STI College with a growing focus on building web‑based and mobile systems that solve real problems. I started with the basics of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and steadily moved into backend development with PHP and Laravel, database design with MySQL and PostgreSQL, and mobile development using React Native.

Each project pushed me to think about usability, database structure, and system flow—not just code.

I care about clean interfaces, logical database design, and software that is easy to use. I am detail‑oriented, trainable, and genuinely excited about growing through real‑world development experience. I am actively looking for opportunities to apply what I have learned and keep improving as a full‑stack developer.
